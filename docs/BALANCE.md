## Balance pass 1

Before this pass (same tool, same seeds) the Hard-mirror table had kaede 26% / boris 29% overall and luiz / meiling 83%. Root causes, found with per-move hit/whiff/damage tallies over `runMatch` batches (both sides of every pairing, 20 per pair, two seed sets):

- The AI, not the roster, made most of the gap. Rushdown archetypes (spacing preference 0.1) only ever dashed in beyond `ideal + 600` mm, so kaede/boris never used their f,f moves at the 1600–1800 mm band where every other character scored 10–12 damage per pick; whiff punishes took the longest-reaching move instead of a launcher; running throws were never thrown (throws were gated at a fixed 1100 mm); and motion inputs cost 3 script frames per token, so kaede's four crouch-dash moves (11 input frames, 70% whiff) diluted her candidate pool. Removing those four moves from her playbook alone lifted her from 34% to 51% in a sensitivity run.
- A sim bug froze a jump attack in mid-air when `vy` reached exactly 0 at the apex (meiling/luiz jump stats): the round then ran out with both fighters stuck, which was the source of every ladder stall. Fixed in `src/sim/match.ts` (airPhysics now applies to any airborne attack).
- Meiling's edge was speed more than numbers: setting her movement stats to the roster norm dropped her 69% → 58% while a 20% damage cut on her four best moves only reached 66%. Luiz's edge was the keep-away spacing plus the longest launcher in the game (d/f+3 at 1625 mm).

AI changes (src/ai): approach only when nothing long reaches (`LayeredAI`), `Playbook.whiffPunisher` prefers a launcher in reach, throws gated by real throw reach (100 mm slack), `notationToFrames` emits one frame per token plus a release frame (f,f = 4 frames, cd = 7), and input delay counts toward playbook startup. Everything stays seeded and integer.
